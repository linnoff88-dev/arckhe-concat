## What it is

Concat is a free video editing program, built as an alternative to CapCut. It is for anyone making Reels, Shorts, TikToks, YouTube videos, tutorials and podcast clips. It does what people usually open CapCut for (cutting, captioning, AI voice-over, background removal, animation), but without charging, without an account and without sending your videos to the internet. Just install it on Windows, Mac, Linux or Android; the AI models download once and then work offline.
